How to Play

  1. Two players take turns: one uses X and the other uses O.
  2. Players mark one empty square at a time on the 3x3 board.
  3. The goal is to place three of your marks in a row.
  4. A winning row can be horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.
  5. If all nine spaces are filled and no one has three in a row, the game is a draw.

Quick Strategy Tips

  • Try to take the center square when you can.
  • Look for ways to make two winning lines at once.
  • Block your opponent before they can complete a row.
